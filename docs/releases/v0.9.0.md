TONFELD is the new name for YTMamp, now a compact desktop player for local music, online radio and YouTube Music.

### What's included

- Local music from multiple folders: tags, artwork, albums and search, without copying your music or scanning the whole computer.
- A collapsible library, 75–150% player sizes and hide/restore while listening continues.
- Online radio search, saved stations, muted availability checks and compatible stream alternatives.
- Local FM-style tuning with Next/Prev SEEK, an optional search hiss and saved city directories. Nantes, Rennes, Menton and Kyiv are included; visited French cities load on demand.
- WAV recording: microphone/dictaphone in LOCAL, station audio in RADIO and explicitly approved tab audio in YT MUSIC, with recording pause, STOP/Save As and recovery.
- Typographic TONFELD branding with the rotated-F symbol. Existing YTMamp profiles, music folders, saved stations, cities and pairing remain compatible.

### Downloads and upgrade

macOS downloads are **Apple Silicon (arm64)**. Windows downloads are x64 installer/ZIP; Linux downloads are x64 AppImage/DEB. Use the matching **TONFELD-0.9.0-extension.zip** for Chromium-based YouTube Music integration. LOCAL and RADIO do not require the extension.

Quit YTMamp before installing TONFELD. Because the name changed, the old app may remain beside it; run only one. Do not delete the original YTMamp profile. Update/reload the companion in the browser profile used for YouTube Music.

The Mac build is ad-hoc signed and **not Apple-notarized**. macOS may require explicit approval in System Settings > Privacy & Security after the first launch attempt. Use that app-specific approval for this trusted download. Windows builds are also unsigned.
